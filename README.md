### 🙋‍♂️ About Me

I'm a 3rd-year BTech student in **AI & Data Science**. Most of what I actually build was self-taught outside the classroom — fueled by curiosity, late-night architecture deep-dives, and turning zero-state ideas into production-ready software.

Whether it's building cross-platform audio engines in Kotlin, training neural rerankers for private search engines, or crafting browser-based canvas editors, I love building end-to-end systems that feel fast, polished, and genuinely useful.

Outside of tech, I also run **Rubal Herbal Shampoo & Oil** 🌿, where I handle creative branding, media production, and digital growth.

### 🚀 Featured Projects

#### 🌊 [UTA-WAVE](https://github.com/aftab125-del/UTA-WAVE)
> **Cinematic, ad-free music streaming & offline audio platform for Android & Windows Desktop.**
- Built with **Kotlin 2.0 (KMP)** and **Jetpack Compose / Compose Desktop**.
- High-entropy cryptographic Fisher-Yates shuffle algorithm with recency memory.
- Bi-directional playlist synchronization, SQLite persistence, and zero-cloud direct audio streaming.
- **Tech:** `Kotlin` `Jetpack Compose` `KMP` `SQLite` `InnerTube API` `Coroutines`

#### 🧠 [NeuralSearch](https://github.com/aftab125-del/neural-search-engine)
> **Privacy-first, ad-free web search engine with on-device neural reranking.**
- Strips invasive telemetry, UTM surveillance parameters, and commercial ad trackers.
- Features real-time on-device **Cross-Encoder Neural Reranking** for semantic relevance.
- Unmasks redirects and proxies search queries without cookies or fingerprinting.
- **Tech:** `Python` `FastAPI` `ONNX Runtime` `Transformers` `BeautifulSoup`

#### 🌸 [TempTree (桜の物語)](https://github.com/aftab125-del/TempTree)
> **Curated aesthetic Instagram Story template studio & in-browser visual canvas editor.**
- Interactive browser editor with magnetic snapping, layer manipulation, and live photo slotting.
- Curated presets spanning Y2K, Minimal, Dreamy, and Vintage aesthetics.
- High-performance, pixel-perfect 1080×1920 image rendering with Sharp.
- **Tech:** `Next.js 14` `TypeScript` `Fabric.js` `Tailwind CSS` `Sharp`

#### 🌌 [UTA-VERSE](https://github.com/aftab125-del/UTA-VERSE)
> **Cinematic web music streaming platform with instant zero-latency playback.**
- Native Next.js App Router streaming proxy with 206 Partial Content range delivery.
- Persistent dock playback with IndexedDB repeat caching for 0ms track reloads.
- **Tech:** `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `IndexedDB` `Web Audio API`
