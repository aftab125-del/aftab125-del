### 🙋‍♂️ About Me

I'm a 3rd year BTech student in AI & Data Science, but most of what I actually build I taught myself outside the classroom — just curiosity, YouTube tutorials, and a lot of debugging at 2 AM. I love taking an idea and pushing it all the way from "just a thought" to something live and working.

Outside of code, I also run **Rubal Herbal Shampoo & Oil** 🌿, a herbal hair care brand, where I handle the marketing and promotional content — so I get to flex both the builder side and the creative side.

---

### 🚀 What I'm Working On

- 🎵 **NebulaMusic (AftTunes)** — a mobile music app (React Native + Expo) with YouTube-based streaming, Supabase auth, and cloud sync
- 🌌 **UTA-VERSE** — a cinematic web music platform (Next.js on Vercel) backed by a custom Express server on Northflank, with live YouTube search & audio resolution
- 🌿 **Rubal Herbal Shampoo & Oil** — marketing & content for my herbal hair care brand
